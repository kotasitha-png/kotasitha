RESEARCH INTEREST

Trustworthy, Adaptive & Intelligent Data Systems

CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION

How can we build trustworthy and adaptive AI-driven data systems that can reason over noisy and evolving data, detect and remediate data-quality problems, verify whether their decisions are semantically correct, explain the evidence behind those decisions, and recognize uncertainty well enough to know when not to act?

Intelligent Data Systems

Exploring how AI and machine learning can augment traditional data-management systems with contextual reasoning and adaptive behavior.

Trustworthy AI for Data Engineering

Investigating verification, explainability, uncertainty and evidence-grounded reasoning for AI-assisted data operations.

Adaptive Data Quality

Studying data-quality systems that can operate beyond static validation rules and respond to evolving schemas, distributions and data conditions.

Human-AI Data Systems

Exploring confidence-aware AI systems that can distinguish between actions that can be automated safely and decisions that require human review.
